Privacy Policy

How Ampere Electric collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Ampere Electric
Gran Exito, Office No 212, Above Axis Bank, 2nd Floor, B1 Kawade Road, Ghorpadi, Pune – 411001
Last updated: January 2026  |  Effective: January 2026

Summary: We collect only the information you provide to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for unsolicited marketing. We protect it using industry-standard measures.

1. Who We Are

Ampere Electric ("we", "us", "our") is an electrical equipment supplier and service company based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. Our website is www.ampereelectric.in (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this Site.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry form on this Site, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your company name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your service or product requirement
  • Any additional information you choose to include in the message field

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, standard web server logs may automatically record:

  • Your IP address (anonymised)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL

We use this information only for website performance monitoring and do not link it to any personal identity.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide through our enquiry form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your specific enquiry
  • Prepare and send you a product or service quotation
  • Follow up on your enquiry if you have not received a response
  • Contact you for clarifications related to your stated requirement

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, newsletters or promotional communications unless you explicitly request to be added to a mailing list.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third parties.

We may share your information with:

  • Formspree Inc. — our form processing service provider, solely for the purpose of routing your enquiry to our email. Formspree's privacy policy applies to their processing: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • Our internal sales team — to respond to your requirement

We will disclose personal information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request from government authorities.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for a period of 3 years from the date of receipt, after which it is securely deleted. If you wish to have your data deleted earlier, please contact us at sales@amperegroup.in.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • HTTPS (SSL/TLS) encryption for all data transmitted through this Site
  • Cloudflare Web Application Firewall (WAF) protection
  • Access controls limiting who within our organisation can access enquiry data

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies

This Site uses minimal cookies — primarily for basic website functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. You may disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting your use of this Site.

8. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023 – India)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where applicable)
  • Nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@amperegroup.in with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Links

This Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as OEM brand websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

For any questions, concerns or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please contact:
