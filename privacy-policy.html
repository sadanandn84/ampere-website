Privacy Policy

How Ampere Electric collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Ampere Electric
Gran Exito, Office No 212, Above Axis Bank, 2nd Floor, B1 Kawade Road, Ghorpadi, Pune – 411001
Last updated: August 2026  |  Effective: August 2026

Summary: We collect only the information you provide to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for unsolicited marketing. We protect it using industry-standard measures.

1. Who We Are

Ampere Electric ("we", "us", "our") is an electrical equipment supplier and service company based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. Our website is www.amperegroup.in (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this Site.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information User Provides

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through our website, such as when submitting an enquiry or contacting us for information about our products or services.

Depending on the information you choose to provide, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Company or organisation name (if applicable)
  • Email address
  • Phone or mobile number
  • Details of your enquiry or message
  • Product or service interests that you share with us

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as financial information, government identification numbers, passwords or payment details through this website.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically collect limited technical information such as your browser type, device information, pages visited and approximate usage statistics. This information is used to maintain website security, performance and reliability and is not used by us to personally identify visitors.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide through our enquiry form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your specific enquiry.
  • providing requested product or technical information.
  • Prepare and send you a product or service quotation.
  • Follow up on your enquiry if you have not received a response.
  • Contact you for clarifications related to your stated requirement.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, newsletters or promotional communications unless you explicitly request to be added to a mailing list.

Enquiries submitted through our website are securely processed using Formspree, our third-party contact form service provider, before being delivered to our designated business email address.

Information collected will be disclosed if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request from government authorities.

4. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain business records and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations. If you wish to have your data deleted earlier, please contact us at sales@amperegroup.in.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • HTTPS (SSL/TLS) encryption for all data transmitted through this website.
  • Secure hosting and content delivery infrastructure.
  • Reasonable access controls for enquiry information.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies

This website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking. Some technical cookies may be used by our hosting or infrastructure providers to support secure and reliable website operation. You may disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting your use of this website.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023 – India)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where applicable)
  • Nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@amperegroup.in with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Third-Party Links

This Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as OEM brand websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

For any questions, concerns or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please contact:
