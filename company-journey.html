Our Growth Story

From Technical Expertise To Complete Electrical Solutions

Ampere Electric was founded with a clear purpose — to deliver dependable electrical products, services and technical guidance to industries and businesses.

With more than 15 years of experience in the electrical domain, the company has built its reputation through engineering knowledge, customer commitment and timely delivery.

Over time, Ampere Electric has expanded from product support into a broader electrical solutions partner offering consultancy, automation, AMC, repair, overhauling, commissioning and after-sales support.
