Our Growth Story

From Technical Expertise to Integrated Electrical Solutions

Ampere Electric was established with a clear purpose: to provide dependable electrical products, practical engineering guidance and responsive technical support to industrial customers.

Backed by more than 15 years of domain experience, the company has developed its reputation through technical understanding, customer commitment and disciplined project delivery.

Over time, Ampere Electric expanded from product supply and application support into a broader electrical solutions partner covering automation, maintenance, repair, commissioning, consultancy and after-sales assistance.
