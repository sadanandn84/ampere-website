Service 01

Motor Overhauling & Repair

Our motor repair workshop handles complete overhauling and rewinding of LT and HT motors of all makes. Every repaired motor undergoes dynamic balancing, vacuum pressure impregnation (VPI) and high-voltage testing before dispatch. All work is backed by a written warranty and a detailed test report.

02
Dismantling & Core CleaningComplete dismantling, old winding removal, core cleaning and slot cleaning under controlled conditions.
03
Rewinding & VPINew coils wound with original specifications, inserted and VPI (Vacuum Pressure Impregnation) treated for superior insulation.

Service 02

Earthing Solutions

Safe and compliant earthing systems designed to IS 3043 for industrial plants, commercial buildings and infrastructure projects. We provide complete earthing studies, soil resistivity testing, system design, material supply, installation and earth resistance testing — with full test documentation ready for audits and statutory inspections.

IS 3043 CompliantChemical Earthing Conventional EarthingSoil Resistivity Testing Earth Resistance TestLightning Protection
01
Soil Resistivity SurveyWenner four-electrode method used to measure soil resistivity at the site. Results used for earthing design.
02
Earthing System DesignNumber, type and layout of earth electrodes calculated per IS 3043 to achieve required resistance <1Ω for HT / <5Ω for LT systems.
03
Material Supply & InstallationChemical earth electrodes or GI pipe electrodes installed with back-fill compound for long-term stability.
04
Earth Resistance TestingFall-of-potential method used to verify achieved earth resistance. Results documented per IS 3043 format.
05
Documentation & HandoverComplete test report, as-built drawing and compliance certificate issued — audit-ready.

Earthing Types

  • Chemical (Maintenance-Free) Earthing
  • GI Pipe & Plate Earthing
  • Copper Plate Earthing
  • Mesh / Grid Earthing (Substations)
  • Lightning Arrestor (LA) Earthing
  • Equipment Body Earthing
  • Neutral Earthing (NER / NGR)

Standards & Codes

  • IS 3043 – Code of Practice for Earthing
  • IS 732 – Electrical Wiring Installations
  • NBC 2016 – National Building Code
  • IEC 60364 – LV Electrical Installations
  • IEEE 80 – Substation Grounding

Why Proper Earthing Matters

Inadequate earthing is one of the top causes of electrical accidents and audit NCRs. Our designs achieve <1Ω for HT systems and <5Ω for LT — fully documented and compliant.
