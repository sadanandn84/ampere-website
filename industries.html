INDUSTRIES SERVED

Electrical & Automation Support Across Diverse Industries

From manufacturing and process industries to infrastructure, utilities and commercial facilities, Ampere Electric supports customers with application-based motors, drives, control panels, automation and electrical solutions.

APPLICATION-BASED SUPPORT

Industries We Support

Product selection, system configuration and engineering support are adapted to operating duty, environmental conditions, process needs, safety requirements and the technical specifications of each industrial application.

Automotive

Motors, drives, control panels and automation support for manufacturing lines, utilities, material handling and machine applications.

Steel

Heavy-duty motors, crane-duty solutions, drives and process-control support for demanding steel-plant applications.

Cement

Motor, drive and electrical solutions for crushers, conveyors, fans, mills and other continuous-duty process equipment.

Food & Beverage

Automation, motor and control-panel support for processing, packaging, conveying and utility applications.

Pharmaceutical

Automation, process-control and electrical support selected according to equipment duty, operating conditions and project specifications.

Chemical

Motors, drives, control panels and process-automation support for pumps, mixers, utilities and related chemical-process equipment.

Water & Wastewater

Pump automation, MCC panels, VFD systems and monitoring support for water treatment, distribution and wastewater applications.

OEM Machinery

Application-based motors, drives, control panels and automation components for OEM machines and packaged equipment.

Infrastructure

Electrical distribution, backup power, control-panel and automation support for infrastructure and utility requirements.

Commercial Buildings

Electrical distribution, motor-control and automation support for building utilities, HVAC and facility applications.

Data Centers

Electrical distribution, monitoring and control support for critical power and facility-infrastructure requirements.

Renewable Energy

Electrical, control-panel and automation support for solar, charging and other renewable-energy project requirements.
