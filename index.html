What We Supply

Products & Equipment

Quality-certified electrical equipment sourced directly from authorised OEM channels.

⚙️

HT Motors

High-tension squirrel cage induction motors for compressors, pumps and heavy drives. Available in 3.3 kV, 6.6 kV and 11 kV.

🔩

LT Motors

Energy-efficient IE2 and IE3 rated three-phase motors from 0.5 HP to 500 HP for all industrial loads.

🏭

Transformers

Oil-cooled and dry-type distribution transformers from 25 kVA to 5 MVA, IS 2026 certified.

⚡

DG Sets

Kirloskar, Cummins and Mahindra diesel generator sets — silent and open type, with ATS panels.

🔌

Control Panels

Custom-engineered MCC, PCC, APFC and synchronisation panels built to IS / IEC standards.

💧

Industrial Pumps

Kirloskar, Grundfos and Flowserve pumps for water supply, firefighting and process applications.
