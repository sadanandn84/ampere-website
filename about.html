About Ampere Electric

15+ years of delivering reliable electrical solutions across Pune and Maharashtra — built on trust, technical expertise and a commitment to quality.

Our Story

Pune's Trusted Electrical Partner

Ampere Electric was founded with a singular mission: to be the most reliable electrical solution partner for industries in Pune and Maharashtra. From a focused motor supply business, we have grown into a comprehensive electrical solutions provider — covering everything from HT/LT motors and diesel generators to control panels, earthing systems and complete project commissioning.

Today, we serve manufacturing plants, hospitals, data centres, pharma companies and infrastructure projects — all with the same commitment to quality, compliance and on-time delivery.

Our team of certified engineers brings deep technical expertise and a hands-on approach to every project, ensuring that solutions are not just supplied but properly engineered, installed and documented for long-term reliability and audit compliance.
